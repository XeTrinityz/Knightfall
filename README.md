# Knightfall

An in-game menu for Batman: Arkham Knight, built on BmSDK.

It covers the player, the Batmobile, teleporting, the camera, the world, a spawner and an object editor, with four menu themes, hotkeys and a radial wheel.

## Options

PLAYER > STATUS

- GOD MODE. Nothing can hurt you, whoever you play as, and the Batmobile can't be damaged.
- CAN'T DIE. Hits still hurt, but they can't finish you. Falling out of the world still kills.
- REFILL HEALTH. Heals you fully, tops up your armour and repairs the Batmobile.
- DAMAGE TAKEN. From 0X to 5X.
- HEALTH REGEN. Heals up to 25% of your health a second, once you have gone 3 seconds without losing any.
- INFINITE ARMOUR. Armour is full again after every hit. You get a full set if you haven't bought any.
- REFILL ARMOUR. Tops up the armour you have bought.
- NO STAGGER. Hits don't knock you out of what you are doing.
- NO STUNS. Shocks, stun sticks and lasers don't stun you.
- SILENT FOOTSTEPS. Thugs can't hear your footsteps, even when you run.
- UNSEEN BY DRONES. Sentry guns, flying drones and helicopter searchlights can't see you.
- UNSEEN BY THUGS. Thugs can't see you, and street thugs fighting you run off. They still hear noise.

PLAYER > MOVEMENT

- MOVE SPEED and LOW TUNNEL SPEED. Scripted slow walks still slow you down.
- GLIDE SPEED. Cruising, diving and dive boosts all scale.
- INFINITE GLIDE. Glide for as long as you like. Look up to climb, ahead to stay level and down to dive. GLIDE CLIMB ANGLE sets how steeply you climb.
- GRAPPLE SPEED, GRAPPLE BOOST POWER, GRAPPLE HEIGHT and LINE LAUNCHER SPEED.
- FALL GRAVITY. From 0.1X to 2X. Lower falls are slower and floatier.

PLAYER > COMBAT

- AUTO FIGHT. Batman fights on his own. He counters, dodges, strikes and uses special takedowns on enemies who block.
- AUTO COUNTER. Counters every attack aimed at you, group attacks and knife combos too.
- AUTO DODGE RED ATTACKS. Vaults over red attacks and charging thugs.
- AUTO DODGE GUNFIRE. Gunmen still shoot at you, but every shot misses.
- COMBO NEVER RESETS. Getting hit, missing or waiting too long doesn't break your combo in a fight.
- COMBO MULTIPLIER. Each hit adds up to 10 to your combo and fills the special takedown meter that much faster.
- BATSUIT V8.04. The upgraded batsuit early, with fear multi-takedowns.
- FREEFLOW FOCUS ANYTIME, HEAVY COUNTERS, SLOW-MO GROUP ATTACKS and ALWAYS SHOW COUNTER ICONS (counter icons in New Game + and Knightmare).
- ONE HIT KO. Any hit you land knocks an enemy out. Shields and armour still have to come off first.
- SUPER PUNCH and PUNCH FORCE. Your hits on foot send thugs flying, on every hit or only the knockout.
- LAST ENEMY FIGHTS ON, RAGDOLL ON EVERY HIT, KNOCK OFF HELMETS, DROP WEAPONS WHEN HIT and HIDE STUN STARS.
- SPECIAL TAKEDOWNS ANYTIME, QUICK STEALTH TAKEDOWNS, FORCE ENVIRONMENT TAKEDOWNS and ALWAYS ALLOW DUAL TAKEDOWNS.

PLAYER > GADGETS

- UNLOCK ALL GADGETS. Puts every gadget on your gadget wheel.
- USE WHILE RECHARGING and GADGETS IN VENTS AND COVER.
- UNLIMITED DISRUPTOR CHARGES and DISRUPT ANY TARGET. The disruptor never runs out and works on everything without its upgrades.
- AUTO PASSWORD HACK. Password cracks solve themselves a moment after they start.
- LETHAL EXPLOSIVE GEL. A thug with a gel charge stuck to him is knocked out when it blows.
- GEL DAMAGE and GEL BLAST RADIUS.
- ALL FREEZE BLAST UPGRADES and FREEZE CLUSTER RADIUS.
- BATCLAW RANGE.
- BATCLAW ZIP KICK. Fire the Batclaw at a thug and you zip straight in with Robin's flying kick.
- SMOKE CLOUD DURATION and JAMMED GUN BLAST.

PLAYER > CHARACTER

- PLAY AS, SWITCH CHARACTER and BACK TO STORY CHARACTER. Turns you into another character right where you stand, with the same health and speed. Your save still records the story's character, so it loads as normal.
- SUIT, APPLY SUIT and CANCEL SUIT CHANGE. Puts on any suit you have unlocked for the character you are playing, without going back to the main menu.
- NO CAPE and BODY SIZE.
- BATTLE DAMAGE and APPLY BATTLE DAMAGE. Sets how torn up a standard suit is. Your save isn't changed, so the next load brings back the story's level.
- MOVEMENT STYLE. Stand, walk, run, climb and glide like another character. Your fighting and gadgets stay your own.
- LOOK, VARIANT and DISGUISE. Wears any character from the game's showcase over your own, wherever you are. Your moves, fighting and gadgets stay the same.
- WEAR AIMED CHARACTER. Copies the person in the middle of the screen, hat and colours included, and wears them.
- POSE. Keeps the character's own stance, or moves them at your exact angles. Each look remembers its own.
- PROPS. Also wears the hats and weapons the character has in the showcase.
- FIT CAMERA and FIT GADGETS. Moves the camera to the worn character's height and puts your gadgets in their hands.

PLAYER > VILLAINS

Play as one of six villains. Each has their own look, weapons, voice and HUD. Only one is on at a time. Batman's gadgets are off while one is on, and thugs stop calling you Batman. Your save still records Batman, so it loads as normal.

- FIREFLY. Flies on his jets. Flamethrower and grenades.
- RIDDLER MECH. The mech from the orphanage, with Riddler at the controls. Smash, beam, hop back and power surge.
- MR. FREEZE. Cryo beam that runs on coolant, and an ice spire. Needs the Season of Infamy pack.
- JOKER. Shotgun and laughing gas, and he can call goons. The gas needs the Harley Quinn pack.
- POISON IVY. A vine that seizes thugs and wrecks cars and tanks, and flytraps that eat whoever comes close. The flytraps need the Harley Quinn pack.
- ARKHAM KNIGHT. Sniper rifle, twin pistols, a drone strike and a militia squad. His suit and pistols come from the Red Hood pack. Without it he wears his showcase model.

Hold aim, then fire or secondary fire. Each villain's own rows list the rest of their keys.

PLAYER > SIDEKICK

- SIDEKICK and CALL SIDEKICK. Brings Robin, Nightwing, Catwoman or Batgirl in beside you. They follow you, join your fights and do team takedowns.
- SWAP WITH SIDEKICK and DISMISS SIDEKICK. SIDEKICK FOLLOWS YOU and SIDEKICK CATCHES UP set how they keep up.

PLAYER > PROGRESS

These change your save.

- ADD UPGRADE POINTS, ADD 5000 XP and XP MULTIPLIER.
- GET ALL UPGRADES. Buys every upgrade the story has made available and keeps your unspent points.
- SHOW RIDDLER ITEMS, UNLOCK ALL EXTRAS and UNLOCK ALL ACHIEVEMENTS. Achievements can't be taken back, so that one asks you to press twice.
- SAVE GAME NOW. Saves the way the game does at story points.

BATMOBILE > DRIVING

- CALL BATMOBILE. Calls the car, or gets you in if it is parked close by.
- RECOVER BATMOBILE. Puts a stuck or flipped car back on the road.
- FORCE EXIT. Gets you out when a mission won't let you.
- REPAIR AND RECHARGE. Fixes the car and fills its weapon power.
- AUTOPILOT TO WAYPOINT, AUTOPILOT TO OBJECTIVE and STOP AUTOPILOT. The car drives itself there along the roads. AUTOPILOT CAN BOOST lets it use the afterburner.
- REMOTE CONTROL. Drives the car by remote without the gadget. REMOTE CONTROL IN FIGHTS keeps it going when enemies come for Batman.

BATMOBILE > TUNING

- INDESTRUCTIBLE, EMP-PROOF and SELF-RIGHTING.
- UNLIMITED BOOST, ENGINE POWER, TYRE GRIP and RAM DAMAGE.
- BATTLE MODE ON ANY SKIN. Battle mode, with its weapons, winch and camera, on the skins the game won't let transform. The car keeps its shape.
- BATTLE MODE SPEED and DOUBLE DODGE.
- BATMOBILE JUMP and JUMP POWER. The jump has its own key, J by default.
- AIR GRAVITY, AIR CONTROL and MAX SUPER EJECT.

BATMOBILE > WEAPONS

- ARM EVERY WEAPON. Fits the car with every battle mode and pursuit weapon, bought or not. They last until a reload or a skin change.
- UNLIMITED WEAPON POWER, NO COOLDOWNS, NO RELOAD OR OVERHEAT and NO GUN SPREAD. GUN DAMAGE makes the main gun hit harder.
- MISSILE BARRAGE SIZE. How many targets a full-power barrage locks on to and how many rockets it fires.
- INSTANT MISSILE LOCK and PURSUIT MISSILE DAMAGE for the pursuit missile.
- AUTO COMBAT and AUTO COMBAT RANGE. In battle mode the 60mm cannon fires at drones and a full generator fires a missile barrage at groups. In pursuit the missile fires at the car you have locked on to.
- SHOOT DOWN MISSILES. Militia missiles homing in on you or the car are shot down.
- BATMOBILE TAKEDOWN READY. The Batmobile takedown is always charged in fights on foot.

BATMOBILE > DASHBOARD

- SPEEDOMETER, SPEED UNITS, SPEEDOMETER POSITION, DRIVING STATS and RESET DRIVING STATS. A readout with your speed, armour, weapon power and boost or autopilot. It floats beside the Batmobile and moves with it, or stays in a corner of the screen. It takes the look of the menu theme.
- VEHICLE RADAR ON FOOT. The minimap keeps showing drones and tanks after you get out.

BATMOBILE > SKIN

- SKIN, APPLY SKIN and CANCEL SKIN CHANGE. Swaps the car to any skin you have unlocked.
- ENGINE SOUND. Gives the car another skin's engine and tyre sounds.

TELEPORT > WAYPOINT

- TELEPORT TO WAYPOINT, TELEPORT TO OBJECTIVE and GO BACK. Works across the whole city and in the Batmobile. The screen fades while the district loads, the way the game's own Batmobile rescue does it.
- TELEPORT ON WAYPOINT. Set a waypoint on the map and you go there as soon as you're back in the game.
- WAYPOINT AT CROSSHAIR and CLEAR WAYPOINT. Set or remove your map waypoint without opening the map.
- NO FADE RANGE, ARRIVAL HEIGHT and FACE TRAVEL DIRECTION.

TELEPORT > PLACES

- CATEGORY, PLACE, TELEPORT TO PLACE, WAYPOINT TO PLACE, NEAREST IN CATEGORY and RANDOM IN CATEGORY. Every area, landmark, Riddler trophy, breakable, riddle, bomb, race, AR challenge and Most Wanted spot on the game's map, by the game's own names.
- SAVED SPOTS. Ten places of your own, kept between sessions.

TELEPORT > MOVE

- BLINK TO CROSSHAIR and BLINK FORWARD, with BLINK RANGE. Instant short hops that never go through a wall. They work in the Batmobile too.
- SKY DROP, TO THE ROOF, DROP TO GROUND and TO THE STREET.
- GO TO THE BATMOBILE and BRING THE BATMOBILE.

CAMERA > VIEW

- FIELD OF VIEW, AIM ZOOM, CAMERA TILT and BLACK BARS.
- STEADY CAMERA stops the hip sway and the run bob.
- NO CAMERA SHAKE, and FIRST PERSON with FIRST PERSON EYE, which puts the camera in Batman's eyes walking, gliding and fighting.
- CAMERA DISTANCE, CAMERA HEIGHT, SHOULDER and LEFT SHOULDER on foot.
- GLIDE CAMERA DISTANCE and GLIDE FIELD OF VIEW while you glide.
- CAR FIELD OF VIEW, CAR CAMERA DISTANCE and CAR CAMERA HEIGHT in the Batmobile.
- PRESET and APPLY PRESET set several rows at once: WIDE, CLOSE, OVER THE SHOULDER, CINEMATIC, ACTION or HIGH.
- RESET CAMERA puts the whole tab back. The game's camera still does its own thing first, and scenes keep it as it is.

CAMERA > FREE CAMERA

- FREE CAMERA with FLY, STAY, WATCH and FOLLOW modes, and FLY SPEED. Flying uses your own move and look controls, bindings and sensitivity. FOLLOW keeps the camera beside you as you walk, glide or drive.
- LOOK SPEED, and SMOOTHING for slow, smooth shots.
- FREE CAMERA ZOOM and FREE CAMERA ROLL. While flying the arrow keys or the D-pad zoom and lean the shot, and Home puts it back.
- HIDE HUD, for screenshots. FREEZE WORLD is here as well, and both work while the world is frozen.

WORLD > CITY

- GAME SPEED, from deep slow motion to double speed. The game's own slow motion still plays on top.
- FREEZE WORLD stops everything but you.
- EMPTY STREETS and FLIP ENEMY CARS.
- RIOTS EVERYWHERE and RIOTERS FIGHT YOU.
- THUG MOB ON BATMOBILE, SPAWN GETAWAY CAR and SPAWN APC.
- JOKERFY THE CITY, JOKER THUGS and JOKER SIGHTINGS.

WORLD > ENEMIES

- FREEZE ENEMIES, ENEMIES CAN'T ATTACK, ENEMY BODY SIZE and KEEP ENEMIES HEALTHY.
- DISARM ENEMIES and REVIVE ENEMIES, for the enemies near you.
- TELEPORT TO NEAREST ENEMY, PULL ENEMIES TO ME and LAUNCH ENEMIES, with PULL AND LAUNCH RANGE and COUNT. Pulled enemies only land on open ground you can see.
- NEW ENEMY HEALTH, AGGRESSIVE ENEMIES, NO MEDIC REVIVES, NO MEDIC ELECTRIFYING and NO DYNAMIC DIFFICULTY.
- MILITIA PATROLS (NONE, NORMAL or MORE) and DESTROY MILITIA VEHICLES.
- HACK NEARBY TANKS with HACK DURATION, and RELEASE HACKED TANKS. Tanks a mission controls are left alone.
- DISABLE ALL TURRETS and DESTROY SENTRY GUNS.
- KNOCK OUT ALL ENEMIES, KNOCK OUT ALL BUT ONE and REMOVE ALL WEAPONS.

WORLD > FRIENDLIES

- The same CONTROL and MOVE rows as ENEMIES, for police, fire crews and other friendly people. PULL AND LAUNCH RANGE and COUNT are shared with ENEMIES.

WORLD > MARKERS

- A 3D box round every enemy, friendly, vehicle, turret, prop, piece of moving scenery or pickup you pick, in each kind's colour and through walls, with LABELS, DETAILS (distance and health), TRACERS, MARKER RANGE and MOST SHOWN.
- THREAT MARKERS marks every threat the game counts on the island you are on, the same list its threat level shows. Watchtowers, checkpoints and explosive devices are marked anywhere on the island. Command APCs, drones and cobra tanks show once they are near you. Each gets a tag with its distance, and EDGE ARROWS point to the ones off screen. SHOW rows pick which kinds.

WORLD > ATMOSPHERE

- PRE-SCARECROW ERA brings back the Gordon section from before the Scarecrow attack: its colour grading, dusk sky and sun, dry streets with no rain, and no lightning. The other rows still work on top of it.
- COLOUR GRADE puts one of the game's own grades over the city: its photo mode filters, or the Ace Chemicals or Stagg grade. NONE takes the grading off.
- TINT, TINT STRENGTH and SATURATION.
- EXPOSURE and BLOOM.
- FOG THICKNESS, FOG DISTANCE and FOG HEIGHT.
- MOTION BLUR and FILM GRAIN.

WORLD > WEATHER

- RAIN AMOUNT, from OFF (dry and silent) to four times the story's rain.
- STREET WETNESS, RAIN SPREAD, NO RAIN MIST and NO DISTANT RAIN.
- LIGHTNING STRIKE, STRIKE AT CROSSHAIR, LIGHTNING FREQUENCY and LIGHTNING BRIGHTNESS.
- WIND STRENGTH, WIND DIRECTION and GUSTS.

SPAWNER

- CHARACTERS: anyone the area has loaded, with SIDE (HOSTILE or PASSIVE), BEHAVIOUR (FIGHT, STAND, FOLLOW YOU or RUN AWAY), WEAPON, HEALTH and SIZE. RANDOM FROM GROUP spawns a mixed crowd. The PLACE rows put spawns IN FRONT of you, at your CROSSHAIR or AROUND YOU, on open ground you can see.
- Every list is read again each time you come to its tab, and pressing select on a pick spawns it.
- BODYGUARDS: HIRE BODYGUARD brings any fighter the area has loaded to your side, and RECRUIT WHO I AIM AT turns an enemy into one. They follow you, fight whoever comes near and catch up when you leave them behind. ORDERS (PROTECT ME, HOLD HERE, STAND DOWN), ATTACK WHO I AIM AT, BRING THEM TO ME, HEAL, DISMISS WHO I AIM AT and DISMISS run the team. On HOLD HERE each keeps their spot and walks back to it after a fight. PROTECT RANGE, FOLLOW DISTANCE, ENEMIES FIGHT BODYGUARDS, BODYGUARDS RECOVER, UNBEATABLE BODYGUARDS and BODYGUARD MARKERS set how they behave and show.
- VEHICLES: any tank, drone, walker or car the game has loaded, HOSTILE or ON YOUR SIDE (hacked for good, no self-destruct), with BEHAVIOUR and HEALTH, placed by the same PLACE rows.
- OBJECTS: copies of any mesh nearby or in memory, or of whatever you aim at, with SOLID, PHYSICS and SIZE. SHOW narrows the list to a kind of thing, and GROUP splits everything in memory into short lists by name. They sit on the floor by their real size.
- WEAPONS: any weapon the game has loaded. DROP ON THE GROUND lays it out for thugs to pick up. GIVE TO WHO I AIM AT, GIVE TO PICKED SPAWN, GIVE TO SELECTED and GIVE TO BODYGUARDS put it in someone's hands.
- SPAWNED: go to or delete what you spawned, with SHOW to list and delete just the people, vehicles, objects or weapons, and clear the fallen and far away automatically. EDIT IT, SELECT WHAT YOU SPAWN and SPAWN COPY OF SELECTED work with the EDITOR.

EDITOR

- SELECT: SELECT AIMED picks what's in the middle of the screen, with a bracket showing what it would take. SELECTED steps through what's nearby. SELECT FROM and RANGE narrow it.
- MOVE: GO TO IT, BRING IT TO ME, PLACE AT CROSSHAIR, DROP TO GROUND, GRAB to carry or throw it, MOVE and TURN steps that also shift what you're carrying, and STAND UPRIGHT.
- CHANGE: SIZE, FREEZE, REMOVE (hidden, never deleted) and NO COLLISION, plus LAUNCH, FLIP OVER, HEAL OR REPAIR (it rebuilds a broken prop too), DESTROY OR KNOCK OUT, DISARM and REVIVE.
- MODE: EDITOR MODE flies the free camera and edits with single keys or the pad. Click to select, hold to grab, Backspace to undo, and the controls show on screen. LAND WHERE YOU FLEW puts Batman under the camera after.
- UNDO takes back the last change, up to 40 of them. RESTORE SELECTED and RESTORE EVERYTHING put things back exactly as they were. Pieces built into the level can't be moved, but REMOVE takes them away.
- A row that can't act on what's selected is locked. It shows a padlock and the reason, like PART OF THE LEVEL or NOT A PERSON, so you don't have to try it to find out.

SOUNDS > GAME SOUNDS

- SOUNDTRACK. Keeps the game's music on one mood, like EXPLORE, COMBAT or SILENCE, until you pick GAME'S OWN again.
- SHOW, GROUP and SOUND. Finds any sound the game has loaded where you are, thug and story voice lines included. Select on SOUND plays it.
- PLAY SOUND, STOP SOUND, PREVIEW and REFRESH SOUND LIST. Plays the picked sound on you. PREVIEW plays each one as you stop on it.

SETTINGS > LOOK

- THEME, ACCENT, MENU SIZE and FONT.
- MENU X and MENU Y move the menu anywhere on screen. Each theme keeps its own spot.
- RESET LOOK puts this tab back to how it came.
- OPEN ON. SCREEN, or GAUNTLET to have Batman raise his gauntlet and show the menu on its hologram, the way he takes a call. In the Batmobile it shows over the dashboard and the car stops, as calls do there. In the air, in a fight, in battle mode or by remote it opens on the screen.

SETTINGS > GENERAL

- TURN EVERYTHING OFF puts every option back to normal and leaves your look alone.
- RESET EVERYTHING puts Knightfall back as it was when installed. Press it twice.
- SLOW TIME IN MENU slows the game while the menu is open (OFF, 50%, 25% or 10%). Full speed comes back when it closes.
- NOTIFICATIONS and NOTIFICATION CORNER, for the notes that show when a hotkey, the radial or EDITOR MODE does something. They match the theme and keep clear of the menu.

SETTINGS > HOTKEYS

- Any toggle, command or choice can have a hotkey. Highlight it in the menu, press B (Y on a pad), then press a key or hold buttons together and let go. The key shows next to the row. A toggle flips, a command runs and a choice steps on. A key only works one option, so binding one that is in use moves it, and a note says which option lost it.
- Every option with a hotkey is listed here under the page and tab it lives on, so you can change or clear any of them in one place.
- CLEAR ALL HOTKEYS takes them all off.

SETTINGS > RADIAL

- Mark any toggle, command or choice as a favourite in the menu with F (X on a pad). Hold mouse button 4 (the right stick click on a pad) for a wheel of them, point with the mouse or stick, and let go to use one.
- The wheel has a slot for each favourite. Scroll over a choice (D-pad left and right on a pad) to step it either way. Number keys 1 to 9 use a slot straight away. Each theme draws the wheel its own way.
- RADIAL MODE (HOLD or TAP), SLOW TIME, POINTER SPEED, SLOTS PER PAGE, RADIAL SIZE, RADIAL ORDER and CLEAR RADIAL. The keys are keys.radial and pad.radial in settings.toml.

SETTINGS > GAME

- KEEP RUNNING IN BACKGROUND and CONTROLLER IN BACKGROUND, for playing with another window in front.
- FRAME RATE CAP past the game's own 90, CHARACTER DETAIL and BUILDING DETAIL. The game's own values stay in its settings file, and are put back if the game crashes.

## Install

1. Install [BmSDK-AK v0.21.0](https://github.com/Team-BmSDK/BmSDK-AK).
2. Unzip the [BmSDK-AK fork release](https://github.com/XeTrinityz/BmSDK-AK/releases/latest) over your game folder. It replaces `Binaries\Win64\sdk\BmSDK.dll` and `Binaries\Win64\plugins\BmSDK.Host.asi`.
3. Put this folder in `BmGame/Mods/Knightfall`, so you end up with `BmGame/Mods/Knightfall/mod.toml`.
4. Start the game and press F1.

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Xbox | PlayStation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open or close | F1 | Click both sticks | L3 and R3 |
| Move | Arrow keys | D-pad | D-pad |
| Change a value | Left and right | D-pad | D-pad |
| Select | Enter | A | Cross |
| Close | Escape | B | Circle |
| Switch tab | Q and E | LB and RB | L1 and R1 |
| Switch page | Z and C | LT and RT | L2 and R2 |

DualShock 4 and DualSense pads work over a cable or Bluetooth, with or without Steam Input.

The menu hides while the game is paused.

## Settings

Your choices are saved in `settings.toml` next to this file. The mod writes it on first start. You can change the keys there, including the keys some options have, like `jump_key` and `jump_pad`. Close the game before you edit it.

## Other mods

Knightfall changes no game files. Texture, skin and other package mods work beside it.

Another BmSDK script mod can clash if it redirects the same game functions. The ones most likely to matter are the camera update, the gadget buttons and special moves.

## Something broke

Send me `Knightfall.log` from this folder. `Knightfall.prev.log` is the log from the session before.
